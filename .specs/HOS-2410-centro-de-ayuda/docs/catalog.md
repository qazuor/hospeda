## 2. The catalog

### 2.0 Conventions for the tables

- **D-H11.** One screen or flow is one article. An article is shared only when
  the same component renders the same way for every audience in it (§3.2 gives
  the reason for every shared article). Ids follow one naming rule:
  `accommodation-*` = the host editor (`components/host/editor/*`); `listing-*`
  = either shared by all three verticals (listing-states, listing-faqs, …) or,
  when an `accommodation-*` sibling exists, the venue/experience listing editor
  (`components/listing/editor/*`, used by both); `venue-*` / `experience-*` =
  one vertical. The ten legacy ids keep their name; five of them now mean the
  accommodation article (§3.3).
- **folder** follows R-2 (`data-model.md` §1): the single audience's folder, or
  `common` when `audiences` has more than one value (§3.4).
- **anchors** lists the planned explicit `###` ids (`{#id}`). The five section-key
  anchors (`que-podes-hacer`, `como-se-hace`, `que-esperar`, `que-no-esperar`,
  `problemas-comunes`) exist in every article and are not repeated.
  Per-vertical subsections use `{#accommodation}`, `{#venue}`, `{#experience}`.
- **covers (short)** abbreviations: `P:` page · `S:` section · `E:` entitlement ·
  `L:` limit · `St:` enum state · `T:` tool · `A:` activation · `F:` file.
  `PE` = `mi-cuenta/propiedades/[id]/editar`, `CE` = `mi-cuenta/comercio/[vertical]/[id]/editar`.
  Every `entryFor` anchor is implicitly in `covers` and is not repeated there.
  **doc-only** lists emails/crons/states the body explains that have no anchor
  kind in `data-model.md` §2.2 (§7, S-1). `F:<…, to identify>` marks a `file:`
  anchor whose path is still unread (only partner-stats-mentions, U-11). A
  `(`:line`)` after a `file:` anchor is the evidence line on origin/staging; the
  anchor itself is the path.
- **Anchor ownership.** `entryFor` is unique across the catalog. Page and section
  anchors are homed once; the CE section pages shared by two split articles
  (`P:CE/precio`, `P:CE/contacto`) are homed once and the sibling is identified by
  its own `section:` anchor (§4.1). Entitlement, limit, tool and state keys may be
  in several siblings' `covers` (S-20, §4.2); `file:` anchors may repeat.
- **bodyLinks** are the `{{article:<id>:<anchor>}}` tokens the body must carry.
  IL-1 `{{link:…}}` tokens for the article's own pages/sections are implied and
  not listed (§6.3 lists the pages that have no `link:` target yet).
- **«Desde el asistente».** Every article whose screen has an assistant
  equivalent keeps a short `{#desde-asistente}` subsection, even when the tool's
  home is a sibling (§5): the sibling links to the home instead of repeating it.
- **Blocks** in the facts column = "the article must not promise X until gap G-n is fixed" (§7).
- **shots** (D-H10, [`screenshots.md`](screenshots.md)): 0–3 planned screenshots as
  `shotId`: what it shows (user slug, fixture). The user is a seeded
  `<slug>@local.test` account; no fixture means the default seed state; `+` joins
  fixtures applied together. Shots were picked to tell similar things apart first.
  Each shot's `page:`/`section:` anchor is the article's own, except
  `inquiries.bandeja-viajero-no-es` (`page:mi-cuenta/consultas`, shown only to tell
  the two inboxes apart, G-1), `listing-delete.borrar-borrador` (taken on
  `publicar/comer-y-salir`) and the two `choose-plan` shots (taken on
  `planes/anfitriones/precios`, homed in accommodation-plan-features: the button
  they show is choose-plan's subject). Callouts and `alt` are written in Phase 5.
