carnival lights and a distant illuminated float softly blurred behind him.

ACTION, IN ORDER: In the VERY FIRST FRAME he is ALREADY SPEAKING — do not start with a
pause, a breath or a look. As he speaks he gives one small, friendly wave toward the
lens with his free hand, low in the frame, and finishes with an inviting half-smile —
the CONTENTO expression from @######EXPRESIONES#######. When the line is over he holds
the smile, breathing and blinking. He does not start any new movement, and the phone
stays perfectly still in his outstretched hand — no drift, no tilt, no rotation, no
change in distance or angle from the camera.

TIMING, as fractions of the shot — never as seconds, so it still holds if the clip comes
out longer than requested:
0-68% he speaks the line and gives one small wave with his free hand. The phone stays
steady in his outstretched hand throughout.
68-100% he holds the smile, breathing and blinking, with no new action. The phone
remains perfectly still, with no drift, tilt or change of distance.

The phone must never be moving at any point in the shot.

DIALOGUE: warm, clear, conversational male Argentine voice from @######VOZ#######, young
adult, moderate pace, close and friendly, not announcer-like. Mouth shapes from
@######BOCAS#######.

The character (S1) faces the camera and says, in ONE continuous flow with no pause
inside it: [Spanish] Entrá, elegí tu destino y fijate qué hay durante tu visita.

CAMERA: this IS the selfie camera — it stays where it is, held at arm's length, with
only the very subtle natural handheld drift of a real selfie. No zoom, no pan beyond
that.

KEEP UNCHANGED, exactly as in @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES#######: the large
rounded blue head and its silhouette, the floating orange sphere beside the head, the
big cartoon eyes and black eyebrows, the simple flat mouth from @######BOCAS#######, the
white hooded sweatshirt with its logo, and the compact proportions with a large head and
short limbs.

SOUND: distant carnival music and crowd ambience, quiet and non-intrusive. No added
music track.
