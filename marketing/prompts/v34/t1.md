# V34 · T1 — Selfie, el gancho de la semana (tres variantes de fondo, se reutilizan)

Prompt para **Hailuo / MiniMax**, modo *image to video*.
Los marcadores `@######NOMBRE#######` se reemplazan a mano en la interfaz.

Montaje y hoja de corte: [`montaje.md`](montaje.md).

---

**Esta toma se genera UNA VEZ POR FONDO — tres veces en total, no una por semana.** El
guion de V34 no cambia semana a semana (solo cambia lo que se ve en pantalla en T2), así
que estas tres variantes, generadas una sola vez, alcanzan para rotar durante meses sin
volver a pasar por Hailuo. Es lo que hace posible armar la edición semanal en veinte
minutos: el trabajo de generación ya está hecho de antemano.

Fondos, en rotación — **29 carnaval** (base), **16 costanera**, **28 balneario** (ver
[`../../plan-videos.md`](../../plan-videos.md#cómo-quedó-repartido)). Elegir cada semana
el que no se usó la semana anterior.

> **Se pide de 4 s —el mínimo— y se usan 2,5 s** (T1 del montaje, 0,0–2,5). La frase son
> 2,1 s.

```
FORMAT: 3D animated character film, vertical 9:16, one continuous shot, no cuts, 4
seconds. This clip is ONE SHOT INSIDE A LARGER EDIT: only its first part will be used,
so the action must happen early and then settle.

THE REFERENCES: @######ESCENA29####### is the starting point of this video: the same
character filming himself with his phone at a carnival parade ground at night, the same
selfie framing and the same light. @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES####### define
exactly how the character looks and his proportions. @######EXPRESIONES####### defines
his facial expressions. @######BOCAS####### defines the mouth shapes he uses when he
speaks. @######VOZ####### is the voice: clone that timbre, that accent and that age.

OPENING COMPOSITION: A selfie shot exactly as in @######ESCENA29#######: the character
holds the phone at arm's length toward the camera, his extended arm entering the frame
from below, head and torso filling most of the frame, a slight natural selfie tilt, the
carnival lights and a distant illuminated float softly blurred behind him.

ACTION, IN ORDER: In the VERY FIRST FRAME he is ALREADY SPEAKING — do not start with a
pause, a breath or a look. As he speaks he raises his eyebrows with curiosity, leaning
very slightly toward the lens, the ENTUSIASMADO expression from @######EXPRESIONES#######
building through the line. When the line is over he holds the expression, breathing and
blinking. He does not start any new movement, and the phone stays perfectly still in
his outstretched hand — no drift, no tilt, no rotation, no change in distance or angle
from the camera.

TIMING, as fractions of the shot — never as seconds, so it still holds if the clip comes
out longer than requested:
0-70% he speaks the line, leaning very slightly toward the lens. The phone stays steady
in his outstretched hand throughout.
70-100% he holds the expression, breathing and blinking, with no new action. The phone
remains perfectly still, with no drift, tilt or change of distance.

The phone must never be moving at any point in the shot.

DIALOGUE: warm, clear, conversational male Argentine voice from @######VOZ#######, young
adult, moderate pace, close and friendly, not announcer-like. Mouth shapes from
@######BOCAS#######.

The character (S1) faces the camera and says, in ONE continuous flow with no pause
inside it: [Spanish] ¿Buscás qué hacer este fin de semana?

CAMERA: this IS the selfie camera — it stays where it is, held at arm's length, with
only the very subtle natural handheld drift of a real selfie. No zoom, no pan beyond
that.

KEEP UNCHANGED, exactly as in @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES#######: the large
rounded blue head and its silhouette, the floating orange sphere beside the head, the
big cartoon eyes and black eyebrows, the simple flat mouth from @######BOCAS#######, the
white hooded sweatshirt with its logo, and the compact proportions with a large head and
short limbs.

SOUND: distant carnival music and crowd ambience, quiet and non-intrusive. No added
music track.
```
