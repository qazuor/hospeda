FORMAT: 3D animated character film, vertical 9:16, one continuous shot, no cuts, 4
seconds. This clip is ONE SHOT INSIDE A LARGER EDIT: only its first part will be used,

THE REFERENCES: @######ESCENA4####### is the starting point of this video: the same
character, the same wooden riverside boardwalk, the same full-body framing and the same

@######EXPRESIONES####### defines his facial expressions. @######BOCAS####### defines
the mouth shapes he uses when he speaks. @######ACCIONES####### defines his body poses.
@######VOZ####### is the voice: clone that timbre, that accent and that age.

OPENING COMPOSITION: The character from @######ESCENA4####### stands on the wooden
riverside boardwalk on a bright sunny day, framed in a full vertical shot exactly as in
@######ESCENA4#######: same stance, same background — the palm trees, the river and the

and rounded, warm golden-yellow, glowing gently, in the same toy-like style as the rest

ACTION, IN ORDER: In the VERY FIRST FRAME he is ALREADY SPEAKING — do not start with a

line. When the line is over he holds the pose, both hands still, breathing and blinking.

TIMING, as fractions of the shot — never as seconds, so it still holds if the clip comes
out longer than requested:
0-88% he speaks the line, listing with his free hand and holding the glowing bulb
steady in the other.
88-100% he holds the final pose, breathing and blinking, with no new action.

DIALOGUE: warm, clear, conversational male Argentine voice from @######VOZ#######, young
adult, moderate pace, close and friendly, not announcer-like. Mouth shapes from

KEEP UNCHANGED, exactly as in @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES#######: the large
rounded blue head and its silhouette, the green and teal band across the lower part of
