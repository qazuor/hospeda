FORMAT: 3D animated character film, vertical 9:16, one continuous shot, no cuts, 4
seconds. This clip is ONE SHOT INSIDE A LARGER EDIT: only its first part will be used,
so the action must happen early and then settle.

THE REFERENCES: @######ESCENA10####### is the starting point of this video: the same
character, the same warm restaurant interior at dusk behind him, the same framing and

his facial expressions.

HIS HANDS: unlike @######ESCENA10#######, he does not hold a phone. Both hands stay
empty and relaxed at his sides for the whole clip.

OPENING COMPOSITION: The character from @######ESCENA10#######, full body, standing
inside the warm restaurant, framed exactly as in @######ESCENA10#######, hands empty at
his sides instead of holding a phone. Behind him, set tables, blurred diners, plants and
paper lanterns, all softly out of focus. Warm dusk interior light.

THE PEOPLE IN THE BACKGROUND: kept far, small, strongly out of focus and nearly still,
exactly as far and as blurred as in @######ESCENA10#######. They never become sharp,
never move enough to draw the eye, and never turn toward the camera.

ACTION, IN ORDER: From the very first frame he is ALREADY reacting — do not start with a
neutral face. His gaze flicks quickly to one side and slightly down, as if bracing for a
phone about to ring with a call he does not want, his shoulders tensing very slightly,

TIMING, as fractions of the shot — never as seconds, so it still holds if the clip comes
out longer than requested:
0-50% his gaze flicks to one side and his shoulders tense slightly, settling into DUDA.
50-100% he holds completely still, gaze settled, DUDA expression held, breathing and

NO DIALOGUE — THIS IS CRITICAL: this is a SILENT clip. The character NEVER speaks, not

WHAT HE DOES INSTEAD, from 50% to 100%: he is NOT idle and he is NOT waiting. He keeps

complete list of what happens in this part of the clip. He starts nothing else: no new
head turn, no hand movement, no second look, no reaching for anything.

IMPORTANT: this expression is wary and mildly guarded, simpático, never frightened,
never distressed, never aggressive. It reads as "bracing for an unwanted call", not as
fear or anger.

CAMERA: locked off, with a very subtle handheld drift. No zoom, no pan.

KEEP UNCHANGED, exactly as in @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES#######: the large

limbs. He stays a stylized 3D cartoon at all times and never becomes humanlike.

SOUND: quiet restaurant ambience at dusk — a soft murmur of diners, the faint clink of
cutlery in the distance — kept low. No music.
