# V30 · T2 — Plano entero, el celular hacia la cámara

Prompt para **Hailuo / MiniMax**, modo *image to video*.
Los marcadores `@######NOMBRE#######` se reemplazan a mano en la interfaz.

Montaje y hoja de corte: [`montaje.md`](montaje.md).

---

Sin diálogo: es un beat visual, igual que T2 en V9. Es la toma que da el golpe de energía
después del primer plano y abre el portal hacia la pantalla.

> **Se pide de 4 s —el mínimo— y se usan 1,5 s** (T2 del montaje, 2,0–3,5). Vale la pena
> probar pidiendo menos: es la toma que menos necesita.
>
> ⚠️ **Es la toma con más riesgo de que el modelo improvise**: no tiene frase, así que
> todo lo no asignado queda como hueco, y con una cara mirando a cámara lo primero que un
> modelo inventa es que hable. Por eso el bloque `NO DIALOGUE` es estricto y la lista de
> qué hace mientras sostiene está cerrada.

```
FORMAT: 3D animated character film, vertical 9:16, one continuous shot, no cuts, 4
seconds. This clip is ONE SHOT INSIDE A LARGER EDIT: only its first part will be used, so
the action must be fast and finish early.

THE REFERENCES: @######ESCENA5####### is the starting point of this video: the same
character, the same palm grove path, the same full-body framing and the same light.
@######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES####### define exactly how the character looks
and his proportions. @######EXPRESIONES####### defines his facial expressions.
@######PANTALLA####### is a real screenshot of a website open in a mobile browser: it is
exactly what is displayed on the phone screen, and it must be reproduced faithfully, never
redesigned or reinvented.

OPENING COMPOSITION: The character from @######ESCENA5####### stands on a dirt path among
tall yatay palms, on a bright day, framed in a full vertical shot exactly as in
@######ESCENA5#######, holding a smartphone up in one hand with its screen facing the
camera and showing the website from @######PANTALLA#######. Pastureland, water and more
palms blurred far behind him.

ACTION, IN ORDER: From the very first frame he pushes the phone toward the camera in ONE
single fast, continuous movement, at an even speed, without pausing or stopping halfway,
while his expression opens into a wide confident smile. The phone comes to rest in
close-up, large in the frame but WITH THE PHONE'S OWN EDGES STILL VISIBLE on all four
sides and a strip of the blurred palm grove showing around it. It never covers the frame
completely. Once the phone has arrived, everything holds: the phone stays perfectly still
and he keeps smiling, breathing and blinking behind it. He does not pull the phone back and
does not start any new movement.

TIMING, as fractions of the shot — never as seconds, so it still holds if the clip comes
out longer than requested:
0-30% the phone travels toward the camera and ARRIVES, fully stopped.
30-100% the phone is held completely still in close-up. No drift, no creeping forward, no
scale change.

The phone must never still be moving after 30%.

NO DIALOGUE — THIS IS CRITICAL: this is a SILENT clip. The character NEVER speaks, not
once, not even a single word or syllable, at any point from the first frame to the last.
His mouth stays CLOSED in a smile for the entire duration and its shape never changes. Do
not open his mouth, do not animate any mouth shape, do not add lip movement, do not add any
voice, narration, whisper, breath sound or vocalisation of any kind. There is no voice in
this clip at all.

WHAT HE DOES INSTEAD, from 30% to 100%: he is NOT idle and he is NOT waiting. He holds the
phone up steadily with both the phone and the framing completely still, and he keeps the
moment alive only with these small things: he blinks twice at an unhurried natural rhythm,
his shoulders rise and fall once with a calm breath, and his smile widens very slightly.
That is the complete list of what happens in this part of the clip. He starts nothing else:
no new gesture, no head turn, no step, no lean, no look away, no second movement of the
phone, no reaction. The shot simply holds on his confident smile until it ends.

THE PHONE: its shape, proportions and screen aspect ratio are exactly those of the phone in
@######ESCENA5#######. Do not reshape it and do not substitute a different phone model.

THE PHONE SCREEN: it always shows the website from @######PANTALLA####### — the same
layout, colours, logo, headline and search fields, in the same positions, sharp and free of
glare. Never redesign it and never invent a generic app screen.

CAMERA: locked off, with a very subtle handheld drift. The phone moves toward the camera;
the camera itself never moves, zooms or pans.

KEEP UNCHANGED, exactly as in @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES#######: the large
rounded blue head and its silhouette, the green and teal band across the lower part of the
face, the floating orange sphere beside the head and its separation from it, the big
cartoon eyes and black eyebrows, the white hooded sweatshirt with its logo — the symbol AND
the word hospeda below it — the white sneakers, and the compact proportions with a large
head and short limbs.

SOUND: quiet outdoor ambience, distant birds. No music.
```
