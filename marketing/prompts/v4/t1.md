Montaje y hoja de corte: [`montaje.md`](montaje.md).

---

Sin diálogo: patrón C, reacción sin lip sync. La narración vive en `voz1.md` y se pega
encima en edición.

> **Se pide de 6 s y se usan 5,0 s** (T1 del montaje, 0,0–5,0).
>
> ⚠️ **Riesgo de que el modelo improvise habla**: no tiene frase, y con una cara mirando
> a cámara lo primero que un modelo inventa es que hable. Por eso el bloque `NO DIALOGUE`
> y la lista cerrada de lo que hace mientras sostiene.

```
FORMAT: 3D animated character film, vertical 9:16, one continuous shot, no cuts, 6
seconds. This clip is ONE SHOT INSIDE A LARGER EDIT: only its first part will be used,
so the action must happen early and then settle.

THE REFERENCES: @######ESCENA17####### is the starting point of this video: the same
character, the same soft blurred exterior background, the same close framing and the
same light. @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES####### define exactly how the
character looks and his proportions. @######EXPRESIONES####### defines his facial
expressions. @######ACCIONES####### defines his body poses.

OPENING COMPOSITION: A tight close-up of the character from @######ESCENA17#######, head
and shoulders only, filling most of the vertical frame exactly as in
@######ESCENA17#######. Behind him, a completely blurred exterior background, green and
water tones. The floating orange sphere stays beside his head, separated from it.

ACTION, IN ORDER: From the very first frame he is ALREADY reacting — do not start with a
neutral face. He brings both hands up to the sides of his head, fingers spread, in the
exact overwhelmed gesture labelled AGOBIADO in @######ACCIONES#######, while his face
shows the AGOBIO expression from @######EXPRESIONES#######: eyebrows raised in the
middle and pulled down at the outer corners, small downturned mouth, big eyes. His
shoulders hunch slightly as if something is piling up around him. Once both hands have
reached the sides of his head, he holds that exact pose, breathing and blinking, the
overwhelmed expression staying on his face.

TIMING, as fractions of the shot — never as seconds, so it still holds if the clip comes
out longer than requested:
0-55% he raises both hands to the sides of his head and his face settles into the
overwhelmed AGOBIO expression.
55-100% he holds the pose completely: hands at his head, expression held, breathing and
blinking, with no new action.

NO DIALOGUE — THIS IS CRITICAL: this is a SILENT clip. The character NEVER speaks, not
once, not even a single word or syllable, at any point from the first frame to the last.
His mouth stays in the small AGOBIO shape for the entire duration and its shape never
changes into a talking motion. Do not open his mouth to speak, do not animate any mouth
shape, do not add lip movement, do not add any voice, narration, whisper, breath sound
or vocalisation of any kind. There is no voice in this clip at all.

WHAT HE DOES INSTEAD, from 55% to 100%: he is NOT idle and he is NOT waiting. He keeps
the moment alive only with these small things: he blinks twice at an unhurried natural
rhythm, his shoulders rise and fall once with a tense, shallow breath, and his eyebrows
tighten very slightly further. That is the complete list of what happens in this part of
the clip. He starts nothing else: no new gesture, no head shake, no look away, no second
movement of the hands.

IMPORTANT: this expression is overwhelmed and simpático, never angry, never distressed,
never aggressive. It reads as "too much information at once", not as panic or anger.

CAMERA: locked off, with a very subtle handheld drift. No zoom, no pan.

KEEP UNCHANGED, exactly as in @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES#######: the large
rounded blue head and its silhouette, the green and teal band across the lower part of
the face, the floating orange sphere beside the head and its separation from it, the big
cartoon eyes and black eyebrows, the white hooded sweatshirt with its logo, and the
compact proportions with a large head and short limbs. He stays a stylized 3D cartoon at
all times and never becomes humanlike.

SOUND: quiet ambience. No music.
```
