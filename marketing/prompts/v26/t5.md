TIMING, as fractions of the shot — never as seconds, so it still holds if the clip comes

0-35% he opens both hands toward the table and nods once.
35-100% he holds the wide smile, hands still open on the table, breathing and blinking.

NO DIALOGUE — THIS IS CRITICAL: this is a SILENT clip. The character NEVER speaks, not

not open his mouth, do not animate any mouth shape, do not add lip movement, do not add
any voice, narration, whisper, breath sound or vocalisation of any kind. There is no voice
in this clip at all.

WHAT HE DOES INSTEAD, from 35% to 100%: he is NOT idle and he is NOT waiting. He holds the

rise and fall once with a calm breath, and his smile widens very slightly. That is the
complete list of what happens in this part of the clip. He starts nothing else: no new
gesture, no head turn, no lean, no look away, no second nod. The shot simply holds on his
satisfied smile until it ends.

CAMERA: locked off, with a very subtle handheld drift. No zoom, no pan.

KEEP UNCHANGED, exactly as in @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES#######: the large
