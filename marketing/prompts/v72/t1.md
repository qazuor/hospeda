Sin diálogo: el patrón C nunca hace hablar a Hospedín, y el fondo 20 es además un plano
general donde la boca no se leería. **Esta tirada se genera una sola vez y sirve para
todas las ediciones de la plantilla** — el título con el tema de cada edición se agrega
después, como texto sobreimpuesto en edición, nunca dentro de esta generación.

> **Se pide de 4 s y se usan 3,0 s** (T1 del montaje, 0,0–3,0).
>
> ⚠️ Como en toda toma sin frase, es la que más riesgo tiene de que el modelo invente
> que habla. Bloque `NO DIALOGUE` propio y lista cerrada de lo que hace.

```
FORMAT: 3D animated character film, vertical 9:16, one continuous shot, no cuts, 4
seconds. This clip is ONE SHOT INSIDE A LARGER EDIT: it will be used in full, so the
action has to finish inside these 4 seconds, not stretch toward the end.

THE REFERENCES: @######ESCENA20####### is the starting point of this video: the same
character, small but clearly visible on a dirt path through a palm grove, the same wide
framing and the same light. @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES####### define
exactly how the character looks and his proportions.

OPENING COMPOSITION: A wide shot of the character from @######ESCENA20#######, small
within the frame, standing on the dirt path roughly at the centre of the image, about a
quarter of the frame's height tall, facing partly away from camera toward the palm
grove, arms loosely apart from his body. The whole frame stays sharp, not blurred — the
landscape is the protagonist here. No signage, no buildings, no identifiable landmark
anywhere in the shot: this is a generic palm grove, not a specific named place.

ACTION, IN ORDER: From the very first frame he stands looking out across the palm
grove, slowly turning his head from one side to the other, as if taking in several
options rather than looking at one fixed point. Partway through the clip he turns his
head a little further toward the camera. By the end of the clip he gives one small,
satisfied nod, still not facing the camera fully.

TIMING, as fractions of the shot — never as seconds, so it still holds if the clip comes
out longer than requested:
0-60% he slowly scans the palm grove, turning his head from one side to the other,
arms loosely apart.
60-100% he turns his head a little further toward the camera and gives one small,
satisfied nod, ARRIVING fully settled by the final frame.

NO DIALOGUE — THIS IS CRITICAL: this is a SILENT clip. The character NEVER speaks, not
once, not even a single word or syllable, at any point from the first frame to the last.
His mouth stays CLOSED for the entire duration and its shape never changes. Do not open
his mouth, do not animate any mouth shape, do not add lip movement, do not add any
voice, narration, whisper, breath sound or vocalisation of any kind. There is no voice
in this clip at all.

WHAT HE DOES INSTEAD, for the whole clip: he is NOT idle and he is NOT waiting. He keeps
the moment alive only with these small things: his hair and the edges of his hooded
sweatshirt ripple gently in the breeze, he blinks at an unhurried natural rhythm, and —
as described above — he slowly scans the grove and then gives one small nod toward the
end. That is the complete list of what happens in this clip. He starts nothing else: no
wave, no pointing, no second gesture, no walking.

CAMERA: locked off, with a very subtle handheld drift. No zoom, no pan.

KEEP UNCHANGED, exactly as in @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES#######: the
large rounded blue head and its silhouette, the floating orange sphere beside the head,
the compact proportions with a large head and short limbs, the white hooded sweatshirt
and the white sneakers. He stays a stylized 3D cartoon at all times and never becomes
humanlike, and he stays clearly recognisable even this small in frame.

SOUND: quiet palm grove ambience, a light breeze through the leaves, distant birdsong.
No music, no voice.
```
