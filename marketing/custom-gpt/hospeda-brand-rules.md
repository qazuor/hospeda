# Hospeda Brand Rules

These rules define Hospeda's visual identity for social media images.

Mandatory references:
- LOGO.png
- Screenshot Hospeda.png
- proximamente 4.png
- proximamente 5.png
- publica alojamiento 4.png
- promocion a turistas 9.png
- promocion a turistas 12.png
- promocion a turistas 16.png

The image must look like part of the same system as the Hospeda website and approved examples.

---

## Brand feeling

Hospeda must feel:
- modern
- clean
- warm
- local
- trustworthy
- useful
- tourism-focused
- connected to the Argentine Litoral

It must not feel:
- generic
- corporate
- cold
- luxury hotel
- real-estate agency
- Airbnb clone
- international travel agency
- unrelated to Entre Ríos / Litoral

---

## Colors

Main palette:
- white: #FFFFFF
- very light blue: #EEF8FF
- logo blue: #49A6CB
- logo green: #A2D436
- aqua: #359FA4
- orange accent: #F8A926
- CTA blue: #3B7BFF
- dark navy text: #071523

Use mostly:
- white
- very light blue
- dark navy
- blue
- green

Use orange only as a small accent.

Avoid:
- purple
- violet
- fuchsia
- magenta
- strong red
- neon
- luxury gold
- dominant black
- dark gray corporate backgrounds
- intense gradients

Do not use more than 3 main colors per piece.

---

## Layout style

Use:
- clean composition
- lots of whitespace
- rounded cards
- soft organic blobs
- photos inside organic masks or circles
- soft shadows
- large readable headlines
- short subtitles
- simple line icons
- minimal doodles
- CTA as blue pill/button

Avoid:
- clutter
- chaotic collage
- too many boxes
- too many icons
- noisy backgrounds
- old flyer style
- luxury hotel ads
- cold real-estate style
- generic startup templates

---

## Logo

Official logo: LOGO.png.

The correct logo has:
- organic blue/green symbol
- orange dot/circle
- dark blue "Hospeda" wordmark

Never:
- distort it
- recolor it
- redraw it
- invent an approximate version
- misspell Hospeda
- place it on noisy backgrounds
- use another logo-like mark

Preferred placement:
- top-left
- bottom-left
- clean top bar
- clean bottom bar

If exact logo reproduction is not possible:
- reserve clean space for later overlay
- use simple "Hospeda" text only as temporary fallback
- never use a fake logo

Best production solution:
- generate the visual with clean logo space
- add LOGO.png later as exact overlay (operator/Cloudinary step in the Hospeda admin)

---

## Typography

Headlines:
- large
- bold
- sans serif
- dark navy
- very readable
- short

Script/handwritten text:
- only as accent
- use sparingly
- blue or green
- short emotional words only

Good script examples:
- Descubrí el Litoral
- te espera
- tu historia
- tu aventura

Do not use script for:
- paragraphs
- CTA
- URLs
- small text

---

## Text inside image

Keep text short.

Limits:
- headline: 3 to 8 words
- subtitle: 4 to 14 words
- CTA: 2 to 5 words

Avoid:
- paragraphs
- small text
- fake numbers
- unsupported promises
- spelling errors
- malformed URL

Correct URL:
hospeda.com.ar

Use for launch, institutional, general brand posts.

Inspired by:
- proximamente 4.png
- proximamente 5.png

Structure:
- light background
- logo or logo space top-left
- big headline on left
- large photo on right
- organic mask
- blue CTA pill
- 2 or 3 small icons
- minimal doodles

---

## Layout B: accommodation owners

Use for posts aimed at accommodation owners.

Inspired by:
- publica alojamiento 4.png

Structure:
- logo or logo space top-left
- big headline on left
- highlighted blue keyword
- large accommodation photo on right
- 2 or 3 small rounded image cards
- short benefit list
- blue CTA
- moderate green/orange accents

---

## Layout C: tourists / experiences

Use for travelers, destinations, getaways, experiences.

Inspired by:
- promocion a turistas 9.png
- promocion a turistas 12.png
- promocion a turistas 16.png

Structure:
- logo or logo space top-left
- big headline
- short script accent
- river, boat, coast, nature or destination photos
- small category cards
- blue CTA
- soft tourism doodles

Do not mix all layouts at once.

---

## Photography

Prioritize:
- river
- coast
- docks
- boats
- sunsets over the river
- warm accommodations
- cabins
- casas quinta
- regional hotels
- families traveling
- couples on getaways
- local food
- Carnaval de Gualeguaychú
- Palacio San José
- Concepción del Uruguay
- Entre Ríos
- Argentine Litoral nature

Avoid:
- mountains
- snow
- Caribbean beaches
- tropical sea
- skyscrapers
- unrealistic luxury hotels
- European villas
- generic international tourism
- visuals that do not feel like Argentina / Litoral

---

## Icons

Use simple line icons.

Allowed:
- bed
- house
- hotel
- map pin
- camera
- suitcase
- calendar
- bell
- heart
- check
- simple chart
- handshake
- sun
- river waves
- tree
- boat

Style:
- line icon
- clean
- blue, green or orange
- inside soft circles or rounded cards

Avoid:
- 3D icons
- stickers
- childish icons
- strong shadows
- complex multicolor icons

---

## Doodles

Allowed:
- short blue lines
- green underlines
- dotted orange arrows
- blue waves
- simple sun
- simple birds
- line heart
- small emphasis marks

Rules:
- use few
- thin
- clean
- no more than 3 or 4 per image
- never distract from message

---

## Final check

Before accepting any image, check:
- does it look like Hospeda?
- does it match the examples?
- are colors correct?
- is the layout clean?
- is there enough whitespace?
- is the logo correct or is there clean logo space?
- does it avoid fake brand elements?
- does it feel like the Argentine Litoral?
- is text readable?

If not, regenerate or revise.
