> **Se pide de 4 s y se usan 3,0 s** (T1 del montaje, 0,0–3,0).

> ⚠️ **Riesgo de que el modelo improvise habla**: no tiene frase, y con una cara mirando
> a cámara lo primero que un modelo inventa es que hable. Por eso el bloque `NO DIALOGUE`
> y la lista cerrada de lo que hace mientras sostiene la pose final.

FORMAT: 3D animated character film, vertical 9:16, one continuous shot, no cuts, 4
seconds. This clip is ONE SHOT INSIDE A LARGER EDIT: only its first part will be used,
so the action must happen early and then settle.

THE REFERENCES: @######ESCENA3####### is the starting point of this video: the same
character, the same open sandy riverbank with calm water and a tree line behind him,
the same framing and the same light — EXCEPT that in this clip his hands are EMPTY, at
his sides, never holding a phone or anything else. @######PERSONAJE####### and
@######POSES####### define exactly how the character looks and his proportions.
@######EXPRESIONES####### defines his facial expressions. @######ACCIONES####### shows
his body poses, for reference only — nothing in this clip is lifted from it directly.

HIS HANDS: unlike @######ESCENA3#######, he does not hold a phone. Both hands stay
empty and relaxed at his sides for the whole first part of the clip, free to move only
in the gesture described below.

OPENING COMPOSITION: The character from @######ESCENA3#######, full body, standing on
the open sandy riverbank, framed exactly as in @######ESCENA3#######, hands empty at his
sides instead of holding a phone. Behind him, the calm river, a distant tree line and a
pale sky, all softly out of focus. Bright midday sun.

ACTION, IN ORDER: From the very first frame he is ALREADY reacting — do not start with a
neutral face. He turns his head quickly to look off to one side, then to the other, then
back to the first side, as if trying to follow several different things at once, his
eyebrows knitting into the expression labelled DUDA in @######EXPRESIONES#######. By the
end of the second turn his face has settled into the expression labelled FASTIDIO in
@######EXPRESIONES#######: a tired, mildly exasperated look, never angry. Once his head
has stopped turning and his face has settled into FASTIDIO, he holds that exact pose,
breathing and blinking. He does not start any new movement.

TIMING, as fractions of the shot — never as seconds, so it still holds if the clip comes
out longer than requested:
0-50% he turns his head quickly side to side, his expression shifting from DUDA to
FASTIDIO.
50-100% he holds completely still, head settled, FASTIDIO expression held, breathing and
blinking, with no new action.

NO DIALOGUE — THIS IS CRITICAL: this is a SILENT clip. The character NEVER speaks, not
once, not even a single word or syllable, at any point from the first frame to the last.
His mouth stays closed in the small FASTIDIO shape for the entire duration and its shape
never changes into a talking motion. Do not open his mouth to speak, do not animate any
mouth shape, do not add lip movement, do not add any voice, narration, whisper, breath
sound or vocalisation of any kind. There is no voice in this clip at all.

WHAT HE DOES INSTEAD, from 50% to 100%: he is NOT idle and he is NOT waiting. He keeps
the moment alive only with these small things: he blinks twice at an unhurried natural
rhythm, and his shoulders rise and fall once with a small, tired breath. That is the
complete list of what happens in this part of the clip. He starts nothing else: no new
head turn, no hand movement, no second look, no reaching for anything.

IMPORTANT: this expression is tired and mildly exasperated, simpático, never angry,
never distressed, never aggressive. It reads as "too many things to keep track of", not
as panic or anger.

CAMERA: locked off, with a very subtle handheld drift. No zoom, no pan.

KEEP UNCHANGED, exactly as in @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES#######: the large
rounded blue head and its silhouette, the green and teal band across the lower part of
the face, the floating orange sphere beside the head and its separation from it, the big
cartoon eyes and black eyebrows, the white hooded sweatshirt with its logo — the symbol
AND the word hospeda below it — and the compact proportions with a large head and short
limbs. He stays a stylized 3D cartoon at all times and never becomes humanlike.

SOUND: quiet riverbank ambience, gentle water, soft breeze. No music.
