Sin diálogo: este video no tiene voz en ningún momento. El logo se compone en la pantalla
del teléfono en edición, igual que en el resto de la serie.

> **Se pide de 6 s y se usan 5,0 s** (T6 del montaje, 15,0–20,0). Es la única toma del
> video y la única con Hospedín.
>
> ⚠️ **Riesgo de que el modelo improvise habla**: no tiene frase, y con una cara mirando
> a cámara lo primero que un modelo inventa es que hable. Por eso el bloque `NO DIALOGUE`
> y la lista cerrada de lo que hace mientras sostiene.

```
FORMAT: 3D animated character film, vertical 9:16, one continuous shot, no cuts, 6
seconds. This clip is ONE SHOT INSIDE A LARGER EDIT: only part of it will be used, so
the action must happen early and then settle.

THE REFERENCES: @######ESCENA24####### is the starting point of this video: the same
character, the same thermal-pool lounge chair, the same seated framing, the same phone
held on his lap and the same light. @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES####### 
define exactly how the character looks and his proportions. @######EXPRESIONES#######
defines his facial expressions. @######ACCIONES####### defines his body poses.

OPENING COMPOSITION: The character from @######ESCENA24####### is seated in the wooden
lounge chair beside the thermal pool, framed exactly as in @######ESCENA24#######,
holding a smartphone in one hand resting on his lap, screen facing the camera, flat and
empty light grey. Behind him, the turquoise water with soft steam, out of focus.

ACTION, IN ORDER: From the very first frame he lifts the phone slightly upward and
toward the camera, turning his face toward the lens, the way the phone-holding pose
appears in @######ACCIONES#######. As he lifts it his expression shifts into a wide,
warm smile — CONTENTO growing into ENTUSIASMADO from @######EXPRESIONES#######. Once the
phone has settled slightly higher and steady, he holds that exact pose: phone still,
smiling at the camera, breathing and blinking. He does not lower the phone again and
does not start any new movement.

TIMING, as fractions of the shot — never as seconds, so it still holds if the clip comes
out longer than requested:
0-60% he lifts the phone slightly toward the camera and his smile grows.
60-100% he holds the pose completely: phone still, smiling, breathing and blinking, with
no new action.

NO DIALOGUE — THIS IS CRITICAL: this is a SILENT clip. The character NEVER speaks, not
once, not even a single word or syllable, at any point from the first frame to the last.
His mouth stays CLOSED in a smile for the entire duration and its shape never changes.
Do not open his mouth, do not animate any mouth shape, do not add lip movement, do not
add any voice, narration, whisper, breath sound or vocalisation of any kind. There is no
voice in this clip at all.

WHAT HE DOES INSTEAD, from 60% to 100%: he is NOT idle and he is NOT waiting. He keeps
the moment alive only with these small things: he blinks twice at an unhurried natural
rhythm, his shoulders rise and fall once with a calm breath, and his smile widens very
slightly. That is the complete list of what happens in this part of the clip. He starts
nothing else: no new gesture, no head turn, no second movement of the phone, no reaction.

THE PHONE SCREEN stays a flat, empty, light grey surface for the entire clip: no text,
no icons, no interface, no reflection, no glow. It is composed after, in editing. Its
shape and proportions are exactly those of the phone in @######ESCENA24#######.

CAMERA: locked off, with a very subtle handheld drift. No zoom, no pan.

KEEP UNCHANGED, exactly as in @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES#######: the large
rounded blue head and its silhouette, the green and teal band across the lower part of
the face, the floating orange sphere beside the head and its separation from it, the big
cartoon eyes and black eyebrows, the white hooded sweatshirt with its logo — the symbol
AND the word hospeda below it — the white sneakers, and the compact seated proportions
with a large head, short limbs and short legs. Nothing about the seated posture becomes
human-like.

SOUND: quiet thermal-pool ambience, soft water and light steam sound. No music.
```
