# V2 · T7 — Entero con inserto, el cierre mudo

Prompt para **Hailuo / MiniMax**, modo *image to video*.
Los marcadores `@######NOMBRE#######` se reemplazan a mano en la interfaz.

Montaje y hoja de corte: [`montaje.md`](montaje.md).

---

Sin diálogo: para cuando llega esta toma ya se dijo todo el guion. Es el cierre gestual,
con el logo compuesto dentro del rectángulo en edición.

> **Se pide de 7 s y se usan 6,0 s** (T7 del montaje, 34,0–40,0). No hay frase que meter:
> es la toma con más margen de sobra de las dos.
>
> ⚠️ **Riesgo de que el modelo improvise habla**: no tiene frase, y con una cara mirando
> a cámara lo primero que un modelo inventa es que hable. Por eso el prompt tiene un
> bloque `NO DIALOGUE` propio y una lista cerrada de lo poco que puede hacer mientras
> sostiene.

**Reemplazos — 5 marcadores, 12 apariciones en total:**

| Marcador | Archivo a adjuntar | Veces |
|---|---|:-:|

| `@######POSES#######` | `../../personaje/poses.png` | 2 |

| `@######ACCIONES#######` | `../../personaje/acciones.png` | 2 |

```
FORMAT: 3D animated character film, vertical 9:16, one continuous shot, no cuts, 7
seconds. This clip is ONE SHOT INSIDE A LARGER EDIT: only part of it will be used, so
the action must happen early and then settle.

THE REFERENCES: @######ESCENA21####### is the starting point of this video: the same
character, the same riverside boardwalk, the same full-body framing, the same floating
grey rectangle to his right, and the same light. @######PERSONAJE####### and
@######POSES####### define exactly how the character looks and his proportions.
@######EXPRESIONES####### defines his facial expressions. @######ACCIONES####### defines
his body poses.

OPENING COMPOSITION: The character from @######ESCENA21####### stands in the left third
of the frame, on the wooden riverside boardwalk on a bright sunny day, framed in a full
vertical shot exactly as in @######ESCENA21#######. To his right, floating in the air at
the height of his head, there is the same FLAT VERTICAL RECTANGLE with slightly rounded
corners as in @######ESCENA21#######, now showing a small, simple, STILL card: the
Hospeda logo centred on a plain light background. Treat the card inside the rectangle as
a fixed image, not something to redesign.

ACTION, IN ORDER: From the very first frame he turns his head and shoulders slightly
toward the rectangle and opens his near hand toward it, palm up, presenting it, the way
the presenting pose appears in @######ACCIONES#######. His expression is CONTENTO from
@######EXPRESIONES#######, growing into a wide happy smile as he settles. Once he has
turned and opened his hand, he holds that exact pose: hand still open toward the
rectangle, smiling at the camera, breathing and blinking.

TIMING, as fractions of the shot — never as seconds, so it still holds if the clip comes
out longer than requested:
0-55% he turns toward the rectangle and opens his hand toward it, smile growing.
55-100% he holds the pose completely: hand still open, smiling, breathing and blinking,
with no new action.

NO DIALOGUE — THIS IS CRITICAL: this is a SILENT clip. The character NEVER speaks, not
once, not even a single word or syllable, at any point from the first frame to the last.
His mouth stays CLOSED in a smile for the entire duration and its shape never changes.
Do not open his mouth, do not animate any mouth shape, do not add lip movement, do not
add any voice, narration, whisper, breath sound or vocalisation of any kind. There is no
voice in this clip at all.

WHAT HE DOES INSTEAD, from 55% to 100%: he is NOT idle and he is NOT waiting. He keeps
the moment alive only with these small things: he blinks twice at an unhurried natural
rhythm, his shoulders rise and fall once with a calm breath, and his smile widens very
slightly. That is the complete list of what happens in this part of the clip. He starts
nothing else: no new gesture, no head turn, no step, no lean, no second movement of the
hand, no reaction.

THE RECTANGLE AND ITS CARD stay completely FLAT and PERFECTLY STILL for the entire
clip — same position, same size, same proportions. The card inside it does not tilt,
rotate, scale, drift, ripple or animate. It is never covered by his hand or his body.

CAMERA: locked off, with a very subtle handheld drift. No zoom, no pan. The rectangle
stays in exactly the same position in the frame throughout.

KEEP UNCHANGED, exactly as in @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES#######: the large
rounded blue head and its silhouette, the green and teal band across the lower part of
the face, the floating orange sphere beside the head and its separation from it, the big
cartoon eyes and black eyebrows, the white hooded sweatshirt with its logo — the symbol
AND the word hospeda below it — the white sneakers, and the compact proportions with a
large head and short limbs.

SOUND: quiet riverside ambience. No music.
```
