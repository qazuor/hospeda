# V12 · T2 — Plano entero, se inclina hacia el celular

Prompt para **Hailuo / MiniMax**, modo *image to video*.
Los marcadores `@######NOMBRE#######` se reemplazan a mano en la interfaz.

Montaje y hoja de corte: [`montaje.md`](montaje.md).

---

Sin diálogo: es el beat que da el pulso entre la frase de apertura y el corte a
pantalla. **Distinto plano que T1**: T1 es medio corto y éste es entero, abierto, para
que se vea toda la galería alrededor.

> **Se pide de 4 s —el mínimo— y se usan 1,5 s** (T2 del montaje, 4,0–5,5).
>
> ⚠️ **Es la toma con más riesgo de que el modelo improvise**: no tiene frase, y con una
> cara mirando a cámara lo primero que un modelo inventa es que hable. Por eso el
> prompt tiene un bloque `NO DIALOGUE` propio y una lista cerrada de lo poco que puede
> hacer mientras sostiene.

**Reemplazos — 4 marcadores, 8 apariciones en total:**

| Marcador | Archivo a adjuntar | Veces |
|---|---|:-:|
| `@######ESCENA25#######` | `../../escenas/escena25.png` | 3 |
| `@######PERSONAJE#######` | `../../personaje/personaje.png` | 2 |
| `@######POSES#######` | `../../personaje/poses.png` | 2 |
| `@######EXPRESIONES#######` | `../../personaje/expresiones.png` | 1 |

```
FORMAT: 3D animated character film, vertical 9:16, one continuous shot, no cuts, 4
seconds. This clip is ONE SHOT INSIDE A LARGER EDIT: only its first part will be used,
so the action must be fast and finish early.

THE REFERENCES: @######ESCENA25####### is the starting point of this video: the same
character, the same low wooden chair, the same cabin gallery and the same light.
@######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES####### define exactly how the character looks
and his proportions. @######EXPRESIONES####### defines his facial expressions.

OPENING COMPOSITION: A wider shot than the reference, pulled back from
@######ESCENA25####### to show the character sitting in the low wooden chair with the
full gallery around him: the wooden railing, the potted plants and a strip of the green
terrain beyond, all softly out of focus. He sits small in the frame, the phone still
resting on his lap with its screen a flat empty light grey.

ACTION, IN ORDER: From the very first frame he leans forward slightly in the chair and
lifts the phone a few centimetres off his lap with one hand, glancing down at it and
then back up at the camera with a widening, confident smile — one single fast,
continuous movement, without pausing or stopping halfway. Once the movement is done,
everything holds: he stays leaning slightly forward, phone held just above his lap, and
keeps smiling, breathing and blinking. He does not lean back and does not start any new
movement.

TIMING, as fractions of the shot — never as seconds, so it still holds if the clip comes
out longer than requested:
0-35% he leans forward and lifts the phone, ARRIVING at the held position, fully
stopped.
35-100% he holds completely still in the leaned-forward position. No drift, no creeping
forward, no scale change.

The movement must never still be happening after 35%.

NO DIALOGUE — THIS IS CRITICAL: this is a SILENT clip. The character NEVER speaks, not
once, not even a single word or syllable, at any point from the first frame to the last.
His mouth stays CLOSED in a smile for the entire duration and its shape never changes.
Do not open his mouth, do not animate any mouth shape, do not add lip movement, do not
add any voice, narration, whisper, breath sound or vocalisation of any kind. There is no
voice in this clip at all.

WHAT HE DOES INSTEAD, from 35% to 100%: he is NOT idle and he is NOT waiting. He holds
the lean and the raised phone completely still, and he keeps the moment alive only with
these small things: he blinks twice at an unhurried natural rhythm, his shoulders rise
and fall once with a calm breath, and his smile widens very slightly. That is the
complete list of what happens in this part of the clip. He starts nothing else: no new
gesture, no head turn, no second movement of the phone, no reaction. The shot simply
holds on his confident smile until it ends.

THE PHONE: its shape, proportions and screen aspect ratio are exactly those of the phone
in @######ESCENA25#######. Do not reshape it and do not substitute a different phone
model. Its screen stays a flat, empty, light grey rectangle throughout: nothing appears
on it — nothing is composited here, this take is thrown away past its first third.

CAMERA: locked off, with a very subtle handheld drift. No zoom, no pan.

KEEP UNCHANGED, exactly as in @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES#######: the
large rounded blue head and its silhouette, the green and teal band across the lower
part of the face, the floating orange sphere beside the head and its separation from
it, the big cartoon eyes and black eyebrows, the white hooded sweatshirt with its logo
— the symbol AND the word hospeda below it — the white sneakers, and the compact
seated proportions with a large head and short limbs.

SOUND: quiet countryside ambience, distant birds. No music.
```
