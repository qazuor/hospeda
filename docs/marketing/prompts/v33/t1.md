# V33 · T1 — Plano general de apertura (fijo, se reutiliza en toda la serie)

Prompt para **Hailuo / MiniMax**, modo *image to video*.
Los marcadores `@######NOMBRE#######` se reemplazan a mano en la interfaz.

Montaje y hoja de corte: [`montaje.md`](montaje.md).

---

**Esta toma se genera UNA SOLA VEZ y se reutiliza en todas las ediciones de la serie**
—Concepción del Uruguay, Colón, Federación, Gualeguaychú y las que sigan—: no depende del
destino, así que no hace falta rehacerla por edición. Es el plano general del patrón L,
sin diálogo: "a esta escala la boca no se lee", así que abre el video sin pretender que
hable.

> **Se pide de 4 s —el mínimo— y se usan 3,0 s** (T1 del montaje, 0,0–3,0).
>
> ⚠️ **Riesgo de improvisación**: sin frase asignada, todo lo que no esté en la lista
> cerrada de abajo queda como hueco para que el modelo invente.

```
FORMAT: 3D animated character film, vertical 9:16, one continuous shot, no cuts, 4
seconds. This clip is ONE SHOT INSIDE A LARGER EDIT: only its first part will be used,
so the action must happen early and then settle.

THE REFERENCES: @######ESCENA20####### is the starting point of this video: the same
character, small within a wide palm-grove landscape, the same framing and the same
light. @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES####### define exactly how the character
looks and his proportions. @######ACCIONES####### defines his body poses.

OPENING COMPOSITION: A wide landscape shot exactly as in @######ESCENA20#######: tall
yatay palms, grassland, a dirt trail and water in the distance, with the character small
within the frame, standing on the trail roughly at the centre, about a quarter of the
frame's height, far from the bottom edge, exactly as positioned in @######ESCENA20#######.
The whole frame stays sharp, not blurred: the landscape is the subject here.

ACTION, IN ORDER: From the very first frame he is standing still, facing the landscape
with his back partly to the camera, arms slightly open in the way the open-arms pose
appears in @######ACCIONES#######. Over the course of the clip he takes a few small,
unhurried steps further along the trail, deeper into the frame, without ever approaching
the camera or changing his distance from it by much. He does not turn to face the
camera at any point in this clip.

TIMING, as fractions of the shot — never as seconds, so it still holds if the clip comes
out longer than requested:
0-75% he takes a few slow steps along the trail, staying small within the landscape.
75-100% he holds, standing still, a light breeze moving the grass and palm leaves around
him.

NO DIALOGUE — THIS IS CRITICAL: this is a SILENT clip. The character NEVER speaks, not
once, at any point. He is never shown facing the camera in this clip, so there is no
mouth shape to manage and no voice of any kind: no narration, no whisper, no vocalisation.

WHAT HE DOES INSTEAD: he walks a few unhurried steps deeper into the landscape and then
settles, with only the environment moving gently around him — grass, palm leaves, the
surface of the distant water. That is the complete list of what happens in this clip. He
starts nothing else: no new gesture, no run, no turn toward camera, no interaction with
anything.

CAMERA: locked off, wide and static. No zoom, no pan, no push-in.

KEEP UNCHANGED, exactly as in @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES#######: the large
rounded blue head and its silhouette, the floating orange sphere beside the head, the
white hooded sweatshirt with `hospeda.com.ar` printed on the back, the white sneakers,
and the compact proportions with a large head and short limbs, even at this small scale.

SOUND: quiet outdoor ambience, wind through palm leaves, birds far away. No music, no
voice.
```
