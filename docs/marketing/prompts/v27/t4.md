# V27 · T4 — En el bote, el cierre silencioso

Prompt para **Hailuo / MiniMax**, modo *image to video*.
Los marcadores `@######NOMBRE#######` se reemplazan a mano en la interfaz.

Montaje y hoja de corte: [`montaje.md`](montaje.md).

---

Sin diálogo: la voz en off ya cerró la idea en T3, así que vuelve al bote sólo con el
gesto — los brazos abiertos que pide
[`patrones-de-puesta-en-escena.md`](../patrones-de-puesta-en-escena.md#e--llega-al-lugar)
para cuando el personaje presenta un lugar.

> **Se pide de 6 s y se usan 6,0 s** (T4 del montaje, 21,5–27,5). Cierre sostenido, sin
> frase que lo acote.
>
> ⚠️ **Es la toma con más riesgo de que el modelo improvise diálogo**: no tiene frase, y
> encima el personaje mira hacia el paisaje antes de volver a cámara, un movimiento
> grande. Por eso el bloque `NO DIALOGUE` es estricto y la lista de qué hace mientras
> sostiene está cerrada.

```
FORMAT: 3D animated character film, vertical 9:16, one continuous shot, no cuts, 6
seconds. This clip is ONE SHOT INSIDE A LARGER EDIT.

THE REFERENCES: @######ESCENA12####### is the starting point of this video: the same
character, the same fishing boat on the river, the same full-body framing and the same
light. @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES####### define exactly how the character
looks and his proportions. @######EXPRESIONES####### defines his facial expressions.
@######ACCIONES####### defines his body poses.

OPENING COMPOSITION: The character from @######ESCENA12####### stands on the fishing boat
on calm river water, framed in a full vertical shot exactly as in @######ESCENA12#######,
the fishing rod resting on one shoulder exactly as before. Behind him, calm water and a
wooded shore, softly out of focus.

ACTION, IN ORDER: From the very first frame he looks out toward the river and opens his
free arm wide, presenting the water and the shore around him, the way the open-arms pose
appears in @######ACCIONES#######. He then turns his gaze back to the camera with a wide,
satisfied smile — the one labelled CONTENTO in @######EXPRESIONES#######. Once he is back
facing the camera, everything holds: his free arm stays open, his smile stays wide, and he
simply breathes and blinks. He does not start any new movement.

TIMING, as fractions of the shot — never as seconds, so it still holds if the clip comes
out longer than requested:
0-40% he looks toward the river and opens his free arm wide.
40-55% he turns back to face the camera, smiling.
55-100% he holds the wide smile, arm still open, breathing and blinking.

NO DIALOGUE — THIS IS CRITICAL: this is a SILENT clip. The character NEVER speaks, not
once, not even a single word or syllable, at any point from the first frame to the last.
His mouth stays CLOSED in a smile for the entire duration and its shape never changes. Do
not open his mouth, do not animate any mouth shape, do not add lip movement, do not add any
voice, narration, whisper, breath sound or vocalisation of any kind. There is no voice in
this clip at all.

WHAT HE DOES INSTEAD, from 55% to 100%: he is NOT idle and he is NOT waiting. He holds the
wide smile with his free arm open toward the river, and he keeps the moment alive only with
these small things: he blinks twice at an unhurried natural rhythm, his shoulders rise and
fall once with a calm breath, and the rod stays steady on his shoulder. That is the
complete list of what happens in this part of the clip. He starts nothing else: no new
gesture, no step, no second turn, no look away. The shot simply holds on his satisfied
smile until it ends.

THE FISHING ROD: stays resting on his shoulder throughout, held by the hand that is not
opening toward the river. It never covers his head, the floating orange sphere or the logo
on his sweatshirt.

CAMERA: locked off, with a very subtle handheld drift. No zoom, no pan.

KEEP UNCHANGED, exactly as in @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES#######: the large
rounded blue head and its silhouette, the green and teal band across the lower part of the
face, the floating orange sphere beside the head and its separation from it, the big
cartoon eyes and black eyebrows, the white hooded sweatshirt with its logo — the symbol AND
the word hospeda below it — and the compact proportions with a large head and short limbs.

SOUND: quiet river ambience, gentle water lapping against the boat. No music.
```
