# V1 · T2 — Plano entero, el celular hacia la cámara

Prompt para **Hailuo / MiniMax**, modo *image to video*.
Los marcadores `@######NOMBRE#######` se reemplazan a mano en la interfaz.

Montaje y hoja de corte: [`montaje.md`](montaje.md).

---

Sin diálogo: es el beat del portal, el mismo mecanismo que T2 de V9. El celular llega a
primer plano y ahí se compone la grabación de pantalla de T3.

> **Se pide de 4 s —el mínimo— y se usan 1,5 s** (T2 del montaje, 5,0–6,5). Vale probar
> pidiendo menos: es la toma que menos necesita.
>
> ⚠️ **Riesgo de que el modelo improvise**: no tiene frase, y con una cara mirando a
> cámara lo primero que un modelo inventa es que hable. Por eso el bloque `NO DIALOGUE`
> y la lista cerrada de qué hacer mientras sostiene.

```
FORMAT: 3D animated character film, vertical 9:16, one continuous shot, no cuts, 4
seconds. This clip is ONE SHOT INSIDE A LARGER EDIT: only its first part will be used,
so the action must be fast and finish early.

THE REFERENCES: @######ESCENA1####### is the starting point of this video: the same
character, the same wooden Litoral cabin, the same full-body framing and the same
light. @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES####### define exactly how the character
looks and his proportions. @######EXPRESIONES####### defines his facial expressions.

OPENING COMPOSITION: The character from @######ESCENA1####### stands on the terrain in
front of the wooden cabin, framed in a full vertical shot exactly as in
@######ESCENA1#######, holding a smartphone up in one hand with its screen facing the
camera, empty and light grey. The cabin, the potted plants and the trail behind him are
softly out of focus.

ACTION, IN ORDER: From the very first frame he pushes the phone toward the camera in ONE
single fast, continuous movement, at an even speed, without pausing or stopping halfway,
while his expression clears from FASTIDIO into a wide confident smile. The phone comes
to rest in close-up, large in the frame but WITH THE PHONE'S OWN EDGES STILL VISIBLE on
all four sides and a strip of the blurred cabin showing around it. It never covers the
frame completely. Once the phone has arrived, everything holds: the phone stays
perfectly still and he keeps smiling, breathing and blinking behind it. He does not pull
the phone back and does not start any new movement.

TIMING, as fractions of the shot — never as seconds, so it still holds if the clip comes
out longer than requested:
0-35% the phone travels toward the camera and ARRIVES, fully stopped.
35-100% the phone is held completely still in close-up. No drift, no creeping forward,
no scale change.

The phone must never still be moving after 35%.

NO DIALOGUE — THIS IS CRITICAL: this is a SILENT clip. The character NEVER speaks, not
once, not even a single word or syllable, at any point from the first frame to the last.
His mouth stays CLOSED in a smile for the entire duration and its shape never changes.
Do not open his mouth, do not animate any mouth shape, do not add lip movement, do not
add any voice, narration, whisper, breath sound or vocalisation of any kind. There is no
voice in this clip at all.

WHAT HE DOES INSTEAD, from 35% to 100%: he is NOT idle and he is NOT waiting. He holds
the phone up steadily with both the phone and the framing completely still, and he keeps
the moment alive only with these small things: he blinks twice at an unhurried natural
rhythm, his shoulders rise and fall once with a calm breath, and his smile widens very
slightly. That is the complete list of what happens in this part of the clip. He starts
nothing else: no new gesture, no head turn, no step, no lean, no look away, no second
movement of the phone, no reaction. The shot simply holds on his confident smile until it
ends.

THE PHONE: its shape, proportions and screen aspect ratio are exactly those of the phone
in @######ESCENA1#######. Do not reshape it and do not substitute a different phone
model.

THE PHONE SCREEN: it stays a flat, empty, light grey surface — no text, no icons, no
interface, no photos, no glare. It is composited afterward in editing; do not invent any
content on it.

CAMERA: locked off, with a very subtle handheld drift. The phone moves toward the
camera; the camera itself never moves, zooms or pans.

KEEP UNCHANGED, exactly as in @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES#######: the large
rounded blue head and its silhouette, the green and teal band across the lower part of
the face, the floating orange sphere beside the head and its separation from it, the big
cartoon eyes and black eyebrows, the white hooded sweatshirt with its logo — the symbol
AND the word hospeda below it — the white sneakers, and the compact proportions with a
large head and short limbs.

SOUND: quiet outdoor countryside ambience, birds in the distance. No music.
```
