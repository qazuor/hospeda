# V32 · T3 — Presentador al costado, el cierre sin palabras

Prompt para **Hailuo / MiniMax**, modo *image to video*.
Los marcadores `@######NOMBRE#######` se reemplazan a mano en la interfaz.

Montaje y hoja de corte: [`montaje.md`](montaje.md).

---

Sin diálogo: es la reacción después de que la pantalla mostró la tabla completa. Vuelve
a cámara satisfecho, sin agregar información nueva.

> **Se pide de 4 s —el mínimo— y se usan 2,5 s** (T3 del montaje, 14,5–17,0).
>
> ⚠️ **Es una toma de riesgo de improvisación**: no tiene frase, así que todo lo que no
> esté asignado queda como hueco. Por eso el prompt tiene un bloque `NO DIALOGUE` propio
> y una **lista cerrada** de lo poco que puede hacer.

El rectángulo gris tiene que quedar **plano, vacío y quieto**, igual que en T1: en esta
toma ya no se compone nada adentro, pero el fondo se genera con él intacto para que el
recorte final sea consistente frame a frame.

```
FORMAT: 3D animated character film, vertical 9:16, one continuous shot, no cuts, 4
seconds. This clip is ONE SHOT INSIDE A LARGER EDIT: only its first part will be used,
so the action must be fast and finish early.

THE REFERENCES: @######ESCENA23####### is the starting point of this video: the same
character, the same palm grove, the same framing, the same light and THE SAME EMPTY
GREY RECTANGLE in the same position and at the same size. @######PERSONAJE####### and
@######POSES####### define exactly how the character looks and his proportions.
@######EXPRESIONES####### defines his facial expressions. @######ACCIONES####### defines
his body poses.

OPENING COMPOSITION: The character from @######ESCENA23####### stands on a dirt trail
among tall yatay palms in the left third of the frame, on a bright afternoon, framed in
a full vertical shot exactly as in @######ESCENA23#######, turned slightly toward the
grey rectangle floating beside him, exactly as in @######ESCENA23#######.

ACTION, IN ORDER: From the very first frame he turns back to face the camera in ONE
single smooth, continuous movement, at an even speed, while his expression opens into a
wide satisfied smile and he gives a single confident nod, the way the confident pose
appears in @######ACCIONES#######. Once he faces the camera fully, everything holds: he
stays still, smiling, breathing and blinking. He does not turn back toward the rectangle
and does not start any new movement.

TIMING, as fractions of the shot — never as seconds, so it still holds if the clip comes
out longer than requested:
0-35% he turns to face the camera and nods once.
35-100% he holds the confident smile, facing the camera, breathing and blinking. No new
action.

NO DIALOGUE — THIS IS CRITICAL: this is a SILENT clip. The character NEVER speaks, not
once, not even a single word or syllable, at any point from the first frame to the last.
His mouth stays CLOSED in a smile for the entire duration and its shape never changes.
Do not open his mouth, do not animate any mouth shape, do not add lip movement, do not
add any voice, narration, whisper, breath sound or vocalisation of any kind. There is no
voice in this clip at all.

WHAT HE DOES INSTEAD, from 35% to 100%: he is NOT idle and he is NOT waiting. He keeps
the moment alive only with these small things: he blinks twice at an unhurried natural
rhythm, his shoulders rise and fall once with a calm breath, and his smile widens very
slightly. That is the complete list of what happens in this part of the clip. He starts
nothing else: no new gesture, no head turn, no step, no lean, no look toward the
rectangle again, no second movement.

THE GREY RECTANGLE IS CRITICAL: it stays completely EMPTY, FLAT and PERFECTLY STILL for
the entire clip — same position, same size, same proportions, same light grey colour as
in @######ESCENA23#######. Nothing ever appears inside it. It is never covered by his
hand or his body.

CAMERA: locked off, with a very subtle handheld drift. No zoom, no pan. The rectangle
stays in exactly the same position in the frame throughout.

KEEP UNCHANGED, exactly as in @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES#######: the large
rounded blue head and its silhouette, the green and teal band across the lower part of
the face, the floating orange sphere beside the head and its separation from it, the big
cartoon eyes and black eyebrows, the white hooded sweatshirt with its logo — the symbol
AND the word hospeda below it — the white sneakers, and the compact proportions with a
large head and short limbs.

SOUND: quiet outdoor ambience. No music.
```
