```
FORMAT: 3D animated character film, vertical 9:16, one continuous shot, no cuts, 4
seconds. This clip is ONE SHOT INSIDE A LARGER EDIT: only its first part will be used,
so the action must happen early and then settle.

THE REFERENCES: @######ESCENA1####### is the starting point of this video: the same
character, the same wooden Litoral cabin behind him, the same full-body framing and the
same light. @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES####### define exactly how the
character looks and his proportions. @######EXPRESIONES####### defines his facial
expressions.

OPENING COMPOSITION: The character from @######ESCENA1####### stands facing the camera
on the ground in front of the wooden cabin, full body, exactly as in
@######ESCENA1#######, holding a smartphone up in one hand at chest height with its
screen facing the camera. The screen is a flat, empty, light grey surface, with no text,
icons, interface, photos, reflections or glare. Behind him the cabin, its gallery, the
lantern on the wall, the potted plants and the dirt path, all softly out of focus. Warm
afternoon light.

ACTION, IN ORDER: In the very first frame his expression suddenly shifts into alarm —
his eyes go wide, his eyebrows shoot up, his mouth opens slightly in a small startled
gasp: the expression labelled SUSTO in @######EXPRESIONES#######. He grips the phone a
little tighter and glances down at it for an instant, as if he just remembered something
urgent. Then, in one fast continuous movement at an even speed, without pausing or
stopping halfway, he pushes the phone toward the camera. The phone comes to rest large in
close-up, but WITH THE PHONE'S OWN EDGES STILL VISIBLE on all four sides and a strip of
the blurred cabin showing around it. It never covers the frame completely. Once the phone
has arrived, everything holds: it stays perfectly still and his eyes stay wide while he
breathes. He does not pull the phone back and does not start any new movement.

TIMING, as fractions of the shot — never as seconds, so it still holds if the clip comes
out longer than requested:
0-55% his startled SUSTO reaction: eyes wide, eyebrows up, a quick glance down at the
phone.
55-90% he pushes the phone toward the camera in one fast continuous movement, arriving in
close-up, fully stopped.
90-100% the phone holds completely still in close-up, his eyes still wide, breathing.

The phone must never still be moving after 90%.

NO DIALOGUE — THIS IS CRITICAL: this is a SILENT clip. The character NEVER speaks, not
once, not even a single word or syllable, at any point from the first frame to the last.
His mouth stays CLOSED except for the small startled gasp of the SUSTO expression, and it
never shapes any word. Do not open his mouth to speak, do not animate any mouth shape for
speech, do not add lip movement, do not add any voice, narration, whisper, breath sound
or vocalisation of any kind. There is no voice in this clip at all.

WHAT HE DOES INSTEAD, once the phone has arrived: he is NOT idle. He holds the phone
completely still while the SUSTO expression softens very slightly at the edges, he blinks
once, and his shoulders tense almost imperceptibly. That is the complete list of what
happens in this part of the clip. He starts nothing else: no new gesture, no head turn,
no step, no lean, no second movement of the phone.

THE PHONE: its shape, proportions and screen aspect ratio are exactly those of the phone
in @######ESCENA1#######. Do not reshape it and do not substitute a different phone
model.

THE PHONE SCREEN IS CRITICAL: it stays a flat, empty, light grey surface for the entire
clip — same colour, same flatness, as in @######ESCENA1#######. Nothing ever appears on
it: no image, no text, no interface, no reflection, no glow. It is never covered by his
fingers.

CAMERA: locked off, with a very subtle handheld drift. The phone moves toward the camera;
the camera itself never moves, zooms or pans.

KEEP UNCHANGED, exactly as in @######PERSONAJE####### and @######POSES#######: the large
rounded blue head and its silhouette, the green and teal band across the lower part of
the face, the floating orange sphere beside the head and its separation from it, the big
cartoon eyes and black eyebrows, the white hooded sweatshirt with its logo — the symbol
AND the word hospeda below it — the white sneakers, and the compact proportions with a
large head and short limbs.

SOUND: quiet countryside ambience. No music.
```
