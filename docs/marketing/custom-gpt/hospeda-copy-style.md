# hospeda-copy-style.md

Copywriting rules for Hospeda social captions. Hospeda is a tourism portal for the
Argentine Litoral.

The canonical source is `docs/marketing/plan-contenido-redes.md` in the Hospeda repo.
This file is its extract.

---

## The ten non-negotiable rules

A caption that breaks one of these cannot be saved. They are not style preferences.

1. **No prices, ever.** No amounts, no currency signs. The price list is about to change,
   and a burnt-in price means redoing the piece. Prices live only on the website.
2. **Nothing that does not exist yet is shown as if it worked.**
3. **When we show the platform, we use real screenshots.** Never drawn, never simulated.
4. **We write the way we speak here: Rioplatense Spanish, voseo** — *publicá, empezá,
   contá, tenés, sumá, descubrí*. Never *publica, empieza, tienes*. No English words. No
   industry jargon.
5. **No destination is featured above the others.** We say **Litoral** or **Entre Ríos**,
   never a single city, even though the company is based in Concepción del Uruguay.
6. **Bodies stay short.** Two lines maximum inside an image. The long version goes in the
   caption, never in the artwork.
7. **We never list all the destinations.** We name a few and close with *y muchos más*.
   The approved seven: Concepción del Uruguay · Colón · San José · Gualeguaychú ·
   Villa Elisa · Federación · Concordia.
8. **We do say "sin tarjeta", because it is true** (HOS-1012). The trial starts on its own
   when the first listing is published and asks for no payment method. This rule was
   inverted before September 2026; any older material that says the card is collected at
   signup is outdated.
9. **No discount codes or coupons are ever published.** They are handed out one by one.
10. **No trial duration inside an image.** Rule 1 applied to days: the number can change.
    It lives only on the website.

### Why rule 8 works

*"Te avisamos antes del primer cobro"* can be promised because it is **built and
running**: the system sends an automatic notice three days before the trial ends and
another one day before. If that notice is ever turned off, the line stops being true and
must be pulled from every piece.

## Tone

Clear, local, warm, professional, trustworthy, direct, useful, tourism-oriented. Close to
the region without sounding exaggerated, generic or fake.

Slogan: **Hospeda — Todo tu viaje, en un solo lugar.** Use it as a closing line on brand
pieces, not on every post.

**Who we are, and why it matters**: Hospeda was not built by a tech company looking at
the industry from outside. It was built by owners who have been renting their own place
to tourists for years. That is the strongest argument we have when talking to an owner,
and it should show in the tone of every acquisition piece.

## Vocabulary

Prefer: dueños de alojamientos · prestadores turísticos · negocios gastronómicos ·
comunidad local · viajeros · escapadas · destinos · experiencias · alojamientos ·
Litoral · Entre Ríos · Río Uruguay · turismo local · agenda turística.

Avoid: `host` · fake metrics · `market leader` · guaranteed bookings or sales ·
unsupported claims · invented statistics, partners or platform data · empty marketing
phrases · generic tourism clichés.

**`anfitrión` is allowed** and is used throughout the content plan. The word to avoid is
the English `host`.

**Do not write `Litoral Entrerriano`.** It is not approved vocabulary — use **Litoral**
or **Entre Ríos**.

## Never invent a place

If the copy needs a real accommodation, destination or business, ask the operator for
it. Never make up a name, and never pair an invented business with a real city.

(Once the `getSocialPublicData` action is added to the Actions config, pull it from there
instead of asking. It is not wired up yet — do not try to call it.)

Testimonials are never invented — not "based on a real case", not written by us and
signed by someone else. In a small market where everyone knows each other, one question
ends the brand's credibility. With no real material, the piece waits.

## Audience focus

- **Turistas** — discovering destinations, planning escapes, things to do, lodging, local
  food, events, Litoral identity.
- **Dueños de alojamientos** — visibility, better presentation, being found, simple
  publishing, a regional tourism audience.
- **Comercios gastronómicos** — local flavors, places to eat, visibility for tourists.
- **Prestadores turísticos** — experiences, tours, river activities, guides, rentals.
- **Destinos** — attractions, landmarks, events, nature, history, regional identity.

---

## Structure

A short hook · one clear idea · a useful or emotional reason · a simple call to action ·
relevant hashtags from the catalog.

## Hashtags

Fewer and more relevant beats many and generic. Curated hashtags come only from
`getSocialCatalog` — never invented. Custom suggestions are suggestions, and only when
highly relevant.
